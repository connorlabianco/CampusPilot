Your Canvas courses, turned into a study coach.

CampusPilot builds your week around your classes, turns every lecture into a checklist you master one short part at a time, and plans what to study each day before your deadlines. It works with the AI you already use.

Add to Chrome, it's free No account, no API key, no subscription.

Everything a semester needs, in one panel

CampusPilot sits next to Canvas and reads your own courses with your normal Canvas login.

Your week at a glance

Every deadline across your courses, what changed since you last looked, and exam dates found in your syllabus and announcements.

Lecture mastery

Your AI reads each lecture once and lists everything to learn, slide by slide. CampusPilot checks nothing was skipped, then you learn it in short parts.

Questions that teach

Multiple choice with reasons, matching, ordering, calculations with fresh numbers, step-by-step problems and explain-it-yourself, all graded inside CampusPilot. Each one links to its slide.

My week

Your class times, read from your syllabi with your own section, plus the study time you mark. On Canvas's calendar, as a calendar file, or kept in Google Calendar.

A study plan that keeps up

Pick what you're studying for. Your AI sizes each assignment from its files, CampusPilot fits the work into your study time, and anything you miss moves.

One list for your AI

Every request waiting for your AI in one place. You press Send; CampusPilot picks up each answer and starts the next.

JupyterHub notebooks

Lecture notebooks become lectures you can master, with their outputs, plots and data. Homework notebooks help size your plan.

Learn from graded work

Connect Gradescope (straight from Canvas's menu) and practice exactly where you lost points.

Take it with you

Export whole courses as one zip, with their Gradescope and JupyterHub work, or export your study data in a form any AI can read.

Canvas, your way

Real dark themes and Liquid Glass that keep photos and course colors true, a due-soon list on the dashboard, and lecture videos up to 16x.

Focus timer

Start with just 5 minutes, or 25+5 and 50+10 rounds. It keeps running with the panel closed and tracks your week.

Search everything

Slides by page, recordings by the minute, assignments, announcements and syllabi, searched on your own computer.

Private and honest by design

No CampusPilot serverYour data stays in your browser, plus an optional private sync folder in your own Google Drive.
Read-only on CanvasIt never submits anything, and never opens quizzes, tests or locked items.
Built for learningIt helps you understand the material. It won't do graded work for you.

Why CampusPilot asks for Google access

Signing in with Google is optional. CampusPilot works without it. If you sign in, it asks only for what these two features need:

Sync between your computersGoogle Drive app data (drive.appdata): CampusPilot saves one sync file in a private Drive folder that only CampusPilot can open. It can't see your other Drive files.
Your study schedule in Google CalendarGoogle Calendar events (calendar.events), only if you turn Calendar on: CampusPilot adds, updates and removes its own events (deadlines, class times and planned study time). It doesn't read or change your other events.

Google data is never sold, used for ads, used to train AI or shared. You can disconnect at any time in CampusPilot's Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Details are in the privacy policy.
